About Me View My Works →

I'm a software engineer and researcher with hands-on experience designing, building, and deploying backend systems and low-level infrastructure across both industry and research settings. My work centers on systems-level techniques that improve the performance and reliability of large-scale cloud systems, data centers, and embedded and edge environments.

Currently, I'm a PhD student in Computer Science & Engineering at UC Santa Cruz, under the guidance of Professor Abel Souza. My research centers on resource management in cloud infrastructures, specifically on the reconcillation of conflicting objectives in multi-tenant environments under dynamic global constraints. My work focuses on the context of sustainability and energy management, treating data centers as flexible, responsible energy consumers that can adapt to the needs of the grid and the environment. Previously, I worked under Professor Gabriel Parmer at the GW Systems Lab, where I focused on improving the efficiency and reliability of embedded operating systems.
